Tiptur Ball Copra Curing Guide:

In Tiptur, Arsikere, and Tumakuru APMCs, farmers cure raw mature coconuts into Ball Copra (ಉಂಡೆ ಕೊಬ್ಬರಿ) over 9–10 months. With modal prices around ₹31,720/Quintal (~₹317/kg), curing yields an implied realization of ~₹45.31 per nut (based on ~700 nuts per quintal), providing a ~1.8x premium over selling raw green nuts immediately.
